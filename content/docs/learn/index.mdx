never opened a terminal, you are exactly who we wrote them for. If you already know some of it, skip
ahead. Nobody is checking.

## How to read these tutorials

**Commands** appear in code blocks. Click the copy button in the corner, paste the command in your
terminal, and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

```bash
echo "Hello, Feature Creeper!"
```

When a command differs between operating systems, you will see **tabs**. Pick yours: Windows, macOS
or Linux.

**Callouts** highlight important bits:

<Callout type="idea" title="Tip">Shortcuts and nice-to-know things.</Callout>

<Callout type="warn" title="Careful">Things that can go wrong, or cost money, or delete files.</Callout>

**Words you do not know** are probably in the [glossary](/learn/glossary).

## When you get stuck

You will get stuck. Everybody does, all the time, including people with 20 years of experience. Here
is what to do:

1. **Read the error message.** Slowly, all of it. It often tells you exactly what is wrong.
2. **Search it.** Copy the important part of the error into a search engine, or ask your AI agent to
   explain it.
3. **Ask us.** Open an [issue on GitHub](https://github.com/feature-creepers/docs/issues/new/choose)
   with the page you were following, what you did, what you expected, and the exact error. Screenshots
   help too.

<Callout title="There are no dumb questions">
  If a tutorial confused you, it is the tutorial's fault, not yours. Tell us and we will fix it.
</Callout>
