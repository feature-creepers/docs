**Bienvenue!** 👋 Feature Creepers is a community of developers based in France. We build apps
together: some are awesome, some are gloriously dumb, and all of them have way too many features.

We started in 2022 as a group of friends who could not stop starting side projects. Today we are an
association with a simple goal: **share the joy of building software**, and help anyone who is
curious go from "what is a terminal?" to "I shipped a thing and people use it".

## Why "Feature Creepers"?

In software, **feature creep** (or _scope creep_) is what happens when a project keeps growing new
features until it is three times bigger than planned. In most companies, it is a problem to fight.

We turned it into a lifestyle.

<Callout title="Wait, isn't feature creep bad?">
  In a company with deadlines and customers, often yes. In a side project you build for fun, every
  extra feature is an excuse to learn something new: a new library, a new language, a new way to break
  production. We know when to stop. (We don't.)
</Callout>

So a Feature Creeper is someone who looks at a finished to-do app and thinks: _"What if it had a dark
mode for the dark mode?"_ And then builds it. And learns three things along the way.
