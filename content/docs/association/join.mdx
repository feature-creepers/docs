## Who can join?

**Anyone who is curious about building software.** Seriously.

- Never written a line of code? Welcome, the [Learn section](/learn) starts from zero.
- Senior engineer bored at work? Welcome, we have plenty of absurd projects that need you.
- Somewhere in between? Welcome, that is where most of us are.

You need two things, both free: a Discord account to talk with the crew, and a GitHub account to
work on the code (we have a [tutorial for that](/learn/github) too).

<Callout title="We live in two places">
  Our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/eXqmagTNrr) is where we talk: questions, help, ideas, show & tell. Our
  [GitHub organization](https://github.com/feature-creepers) is where the code lives. There is no
  other channel.
</Callout>

## How to join

<Steps>
<Step>

### Join our Discord and say hi

Open the [invitation to the Feature Creepers server](https://discord.gg/eXqmagTNrr) and join. Then introduce yourself: what you
know, what you want to learn, and the most useless feature you dream of building. There are no wrong
answers (except "nothing").

</Step>
<Step>

### Read the manifesto and the code of conduct

Two short reads: the [mission & values](/association/mission) (fun) and the
[code of conduct](/association/code-of-conduct) (important). By joining, you agree to both.

</Step>
<Step>

### Create a GitHub account

GitHub is where our projects and these docs live. If you do not have an account yet, follow
[What is GitHub?](/learn/github) to create one and set it up.

</Step>
<Step>

### Get your invitation to the GitHub organization

Give your GitHub username to the crew on Discord (or, if you prefer, open a
[join request](https://github.com/feature-creepers/docs/issues/new?template=join.yml) on GitHub). A
member invites you to the [Feature Creepers organization](https://github.com/feature-creepers). You
will get an email from GitHub: click **Join @feature-creepers**, and you are in.

</Step>
<Step>

### Sign the Wall of Creepers

Your first mission: add yourself to the [Wall of Creepers](/association/wall-of-creepers) with your
very first pull request. The [step-by-step tutorial](/learn/first-pull-request) holds your hand the
whole way.

</Step>
<Step>

### Pick something to build

Join an existing [project](/association/projects), pitch your own on Discord, or follow the
[learning path](/learn). Ask questions anytime.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What you get

- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **A crew.** People to build with, ask questions to, and show your projects to.
- 🔍 **Code reviews.** Kind, detailed feedback on your pull requests, so you learn fast.
- 🗺️ **A path.** Tutorials that take you from the terminal to shipping with AI agents.
- 🧪 **A playground.** Real projects where you can try things, break things, and fix things.
- 🎉 **The official right to add useless features.** Use it wisely. Or don't.

## What we ask in return

- **Be kind.** Follow the [code of conduct](/association/code-of-conduct). Review code, not people.
- **Share what you learn.** Answer a question, write a tip, improve a tutorial.
- **Keep secrets secret.** Never commit passwords, tokens or API keys. Never share someone else's
  private code.
- **Have fun.** If you stop having fun, tell us. We will find you a sillier project.

<Callout title="Not ready to join yet?">
  No pressure. Everything on this site is public: follow the tutorials, star our repositories, and come
  say hi on [Discord](https://discord.gg/eXqmagTNrr) whenever you feel like it.
</Callout>
