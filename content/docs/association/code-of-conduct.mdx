We are here to build fun things and help each other learn. That only works if everyone feels safe and
welcome. This code of conduct applies to every Feature Creepers space: our Discord server, our GitHub
repositories, issues and pull requests, events, and anywhere you represent the association.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and
healthy community.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient**, especially with beginners. Everyone was one.
- **Review code, not people.** Critique the work, explain your reasoning, suggest alternatives.
- **Assume good intent**, and ask before assuming anything else.
- **Accept feedback gracefully**, and give it the way you would like to receive it.
- **Own your mistakes.** Apologize to those affected and learn from it. We all break production
  sometimes.
- **Respect privacy.** Do not share someone else's private code, messages or personal information
  without their permission.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, harassment, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention or advances of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes and language. Our humor is absurd, not cruel.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their
  explicit permission.
- Gatekeeping: mocking someone for what they do not know yet, or for asking a question.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a friendly community.

## Reporting a problem

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have any other concern, please **report it
privately**: send a direct message to an admin of our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/eXqmagTNrr),
or to an owner of the [GitHub organization](https://github.com/feature-creepers). Do not report it in a
public channel.

All reports will be handled with discretion. We will respect the privacy and safety of the person
who reports an incident.

## Consequences

Depending on the situation, the Discord admins and organization owners may take any action they deem
appropriate, including:

1. **A private conversation** explaining why the behavior was a problem.
2. **A warning**, with clear consequences for continued behavior.
3. **A temporary ban** from community spaces.
4. **A permanent ban** from the community.
